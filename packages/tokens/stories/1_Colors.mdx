# Colors

## Contrast

Each contextual step carries an explicit contrast contract against [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) AA (4.5:1 for normal text):

- **`-dark-1`**: at least 7:1 against white. For emphasis, hover, and pressed states.
- **`-regular`**: at least 4.5:1 against white AND at least 4.5:1 against its own `-light-2`. This is the default step: use it for text, icons, borders, and as a fill with white text.
- **`-light-1`**: the light step, for use on dark surfaces. At least 4.5:1 as a fill carrying dark text, AND at least 4.5:1 as text or an icon on a dark surface.
- **`-light-2`**: the soft surface, meant as a background, not as a foreground color.

`-regular` is the step to reach for by default; it no longer requires falling back to `-dark-1` for ordinary text, icon, or border use. `-dark-1` remains the choice when a stronger, higher-emphasis contrast is wanted.
