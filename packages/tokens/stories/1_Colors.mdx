## Contrast

The contextual color steps are not interchangeable as foreground/background pairs. Before pairing a `-regular`, `-dark-1`, `-light-1` or `-light-2` step with another, check its ratio against the [WCAG 2.1](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG21/) thresholds: 4.5:1 for normal text, and 3:1 for large text (18px bold or larger, or 24px regular or larger) and non-text elements such as icons and borders. A step that meets contrast in one family does not necessarily meet it in another; the hues carry different luminance even at the same step name.

The most natural-looking combination, a `-regular` step as text on its own `-light-2` surface, fails AA for three of the four families. The table below covers every pairing worth checking.

### Recommended pairings

- **Text or icons on white:** use `-dark-1`. It passes in all four families (5.23 to 7.68).
- **Text on the family's own `-light-2` surface:** use `-dark-1`, not `-regular`. It passes in all four families (4.74 to 7.20).
- **`-regular` as a filled background:** pair it with white text for success, error and info. Do not pair it with white text for warning; see below.

### Warning is a special case

Warning's `-regular` step is a bright yellow fill. It only reaches AA when paired with a dark foreground: `-dark-1` text on `-regular` reaches 10.24:1. It is not usable as an icon, a border, or a text color against a white background (1.54:1, well under the 3:1 non-text threshold), and white text on it also fails (1.54:1). Reach for `-dark-1` whenever warning needs to read as text, an icon, or a border on a light surface.

No palette value changes as a result of this section; these are usage constraints inherent to the hues themselves.
